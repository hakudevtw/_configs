# Spanish (es)

Native review: none yet.

## Register

Usted throughout, for every sentence including form errors and buttons (Seleccione, Acepte). Neutral international Spanish unless the product targets one country.

## Words and traps

- One word for a vehicle: autobús, not autobús and bus mixed.
- Departure is salida, never despacho (dispatch, an office).
- Avoid calques: piezas de equipaje → maletas; toma el mando (of a vehicle) → continúa el trayecto; escenarios de uso → casos de uso.
- Overnight is con pernoctación, not "de noche".
- Instructions as a list are imperatives in usted (Abróchese el cinturón), not bare nouns or infinitives mixed.

## Typography

- Sentence case for titles and headings (Política de cancelación), not English Title Case. No capital after an opening parenthesis mid-phrase.
- Opening ¿ and ¡.
- Dates 9 de octubre de 2026; thousands with a point (1.000) unless the product formats numbers itself.

## Place names

Japanese landmarks keep their usual romanisation (Senso-ji, Fushimi Inari); regions take Spanish forms where they exist (Alpes japoneses).

## Check before handing over

- [ ] No tú forms anywhere.
- [ ] No title case in headings.
- [ ] No English words a Spanish reader would not use (trade, desk).
