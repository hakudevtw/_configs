# Filipino / Tagalog (fil; some products use the code ph)

Native review: none yet.

## Register

Polite plural throughout: po, ninyo, inyo, kayo. Never mo, ka or iyo for the customer, including in errors and short labels (Dalhin ang Inyong Basura).

## Words and traps

- Taglish is normal: keep the English loanwords Filipino web copy uses (booking, quote, driver, guide, itinerary). A whole sentence left in English is untranslated, not Taglish.
- Pick one word per concept, English or Filipino, and keep it (guide or gabay, not both).
- Number linker consistent across the page: 5 araw or 5 na araw, one form; same for pasahero.
- Avoid stiff written forms such as mangyaring: pakirekomenda po.
- Buong coach means the whole coach; a full-size coach is full-size na coach.
- Day labels match the page (Araw 4–5 when days are ARAW n).

## Typography

- Dates Oktubre 9, 2026 or the English form the product already uses; thousands with a comma.

## Place names

Usual romanisation; "Japanese Alps" rather than bare Alps.

## Check before handing over

- [ ] No informal mo/ka/iyo.
- [ ] No sentence fully in English.
- [ ] Linkers and loanwords consistent.
