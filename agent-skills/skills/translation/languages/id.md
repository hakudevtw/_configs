# Indonesian (id)

Native review: none yet.

## Register

Anda throughout (capital A). Polite and direct: Silakan…, Mohon….

## Words and traps

- Drop-off is pengantaran / diantar ke; penurunan means a decrease.
- Pick-up (penjemputan) and departure (keberangkatan) are different words; do not swap them.
- The language is bahasa Thai; Thailand is the country.
- Error titles in Indonesian (Terjadi Kesalahan), not English "Error".
- One spelling per borrowed word across the page (itinerari, not itinerary and itinerari mixed); prefer the KBBI form.
- Travel agency: biro perjalanan or agen perjalanan, one of them throughout.
- Vehicle names capitalised the same way everywhere (Bus Sedang, Bus Besar).

## Typography

- Dates 9 Oktober 2026; thousands with a point (1.000); times 08.30 or 08:30, one form.

## Place names

Usual romanisation; regions in Indonesian where the form exists (Pegunungan Alpen Jepang).
