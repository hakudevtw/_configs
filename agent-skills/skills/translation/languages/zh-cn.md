## Words and traps

- Mainland vocabulary: 酒店、信息、视频、软件、网络、默认、用户、出租车、短信.
- Not zh-tw put through a converter: check words, not only characters.
- Counters: 件 for luggage, 位 for passengers, 辆 for vehicles.

## Typography

- Full-width punctuation，。、：；！？（）; quotes “” and ‘’.
- Numbers and Latin stay half-width; the space between Chinese and Latin follows the product, consistently.
- Dates 2026年10月9日; times 08:30.
