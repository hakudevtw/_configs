# Traditional Chinese, Taiwan (zh-tw)

Native review: pending (a Taiwanese reviewer, charter-bus page, 2026-10). Corrections land here.

## Register

您 for the reader; polite and plain, never 親 or slang. Instructions as requests: 請選擇…、請輸入….

## Words and traps

zh-tw is Taiwanese usage, not zh-cn put through a character converter. A converter swaps characters and keeps mainland words.

| Taiwan | Not (mainland or wrong) |
| -- | -- |
| 飯店 | 酒店 (in Taiwan it can read as a hostess club) |
| 資訊 | 信息 |
| 支援 (support) | 支持 |
| 品質 | 質量 |
| 影片 | 視頻 |
| 軟體、網路、預設、帳號、使用者 | 軟件、網絡、默認、賬號、用戶 |
| 計程車 | 出租車 |
| 簡訊 | 短信 |
| 一件行李、約45件 | 一只行李、45只 |

- Counters: 件 for luggage, 位 for passengers, 輛 for vehicles, 個 for seats (or 座位數).
- 包車、專車、遊覽車 for coach hire; keep the product's own word once chosen.

## Typography

- Full-width punctuation：，。、：；！？（）; quotes 「」 and 『』, not “”.
- Numbers, Latin words and units stay half-width. A space between Chinese and Latin or digits is a house style: follow what the product already does, and do it everywhere.
- Dates 2026年10月9日; times 08:30; ranges 14至8天前 or 14–8天前, one form per page.

## Place names

Japanese places in their kanji, in traditional characters: 明治神宮、淺草寺、伏見稻荷大社、清水寺、金閣寺、東大寺、春日大社、新千歲機場. A place known in Taiwan by a translated name takes it (晴空街道). A place with no common Chinese name keeps its romanisation with a locator: 神戶港 Harborland.
