Native review: pending (a Korean reviewer, charter-bus page, 2026-10). Corrections land here.

## Register

One speech level for the whole product: 합니다체 for business pages (or 해요체 if the product already uses it), never mixed. Instructions as -해 주세요 / -하십시오, consistently.

## Words and traps

- **Japanese calques** read as machine translation: 송영 (送迎) → 셔틀·픽업; 대응 (対応) for "coverage" → 운행·지원; 다일정 is not a word → 여러 날에 걸친.
- 렌터카 means a self-drive rental. A car with a driver is 기사 포함 차량 / 전용 차량.
- English transliterations Koreans do not use (트레이드 데스크, 풀사이즈 코치) → say what it means (여행사 전용 창구, 대형 관광버스).
- Counters: 석 for seats, 개 for luggage, 명 for people, 대 for vehicles. Put the counter on every figure of a set, not only some.
- Spacing (띄어쓰기) is checked by readers: 하시는 것으로, 동의합니다, 처리방침.

## Typography

- Half-width punctuation; no space before ? or !.
- Dates 2026년 10월 9일; times 오전 8시 30분 or 08:30, one form per page.
- In tagged links, wrap the linked noun only: `<url>개인정보 처리방침</url>에 동의`.

## Place names

Korean loanword orthography (외래어 표기법) and the form Korean travel writing uses: 메이지 신궁, 센소지, 후시미 이나리 타이샤, 기요미즈데라, 신치토세 공항, 일본 알프스. Dishes as known in Korea: 고베규.
